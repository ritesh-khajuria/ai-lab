# PLAN-A Refill Too Soon Policy
Document version: 2026.02 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## Standard Refill Threshold
PLAN-A permits a refill once 75% of the previous days supply has elapsed.
For a 30-day supply, the earliest eligible refill date is day 23. For a 90-day
supply, it is day 68.

Claims submitted before the eligible date are rejected with NCPDP reject code 79
(Refill Too Soon). The reject message returns the earliest eligible fill date.

## Controlled Substances
Schedule II controlled substances require 100% of the days supply to elapse.
No early refill override is available at the pharmacy counter; these require
prescriber attestation and plan review.

## Approved Override Reasons
A refill-too-soon override may be granted for:
- Documented lost, stolen, or damaged medication (police report or signed
  member attestation required for controlled substances)
- Dose increase directed by the prescriber, with a new prescription on file
- Extended travel, up to one additional fill per rolling 12 months, with the
  member's travel dates documented
- Transition of care following hospital discharge, within 14 days of discharge

## Requesting an Override
The pharmacy contacts the PLAN-A pharmacy help desk with the claim number and
the override reason. Vacation and dose-change overrides can be applied at point
of sale. Lost or stolen overrides for controlled substances require review and
are not applied at the counter.
