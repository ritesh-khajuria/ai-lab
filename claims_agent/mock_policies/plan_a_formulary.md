# PLAN-A Formulary and Coverage Tiers
Document version: 2026.02 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## Tier Structure
- Tier 1 - Preferred Generic: $10 copay
- Tier 2 - Non-Preferred Generic: $25 copay
- Tier 3 - Preferred Brand: $50 copay
- Tier 4 - Non-Preferred Brand: $90 copay
- Tier 5 - Specialty: 25% coinsurance, capped at $250 per fill

## Excluded Products
Products not on the PLAN-A formulary are rejected with NCPDP reject code 70
(Product/Service not covered). Excluded categories include cosmetic
preparations, over-the-counter products without a prescription, fertility
agents, and weight-loss-only indications.

An exclusion is not the same as a prior authorization requirement. Reject 70
means the product is not covered at all under this plan; reject 75 means the
product is covered once a PA is approved.

## Common Products
- Atorvastatin (generic) - Tier 1
- Metformin (generic) - Tier 1
- Ozempic (semaglutide) - Tier 5 Specialty, PA required
- Insulin glargine - Tier 3

## Quantity Limits
Standard retail fills are limited to a 30-day supply. Mail order and preferred
90-day retail pharmacies may dispense up to a 90-day supply for maintenance
medications. Claims exceeding the plan limit are rejected with reject code 76
(Plan limitation exceeded).
