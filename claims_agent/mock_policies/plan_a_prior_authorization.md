# PLAN-A Prior Authorization Policy
Document version: 2026.02 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## When Prior Authorization Applies
A Prior Authorization (PA) is required before PLAN-A will cover certain drugs.
Claims for these drugs submitted without an approved PA on file are rejected
with NCPDP reject code 75.

## GLP-1 Receptor Agonists (semaglutide, tirzepatide, liraglutide)
Products including Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro and Saxenda require PA.

Approval criteria - ALL must be met:
1. Documented diagnosis of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (ICD-10 E11.x), AND
2. Baseline HbA1c of 7.0% or greater documented within the last 90 days, AND
3. Trial and failure, intolerance, or contraindication to metformin for at
   least 90 days, unless metformin is contraindicated, AND
4. Prescribed by or in consultation with an endocrinologist or the member's
   primary care physician.

Weight-management-only indications are NOT covered under PLAN-A medical benefit
regardless of PA status.

Initial approval duration: 6 months. Renewal requires documented HbA1c
improvement of at least 0.5 percentage points, or documented clinical rationale
for continuation.

## Submitting a PA
The prescriber submits the PA request with supporting clinical documentation
(chart notes, recent lab values, medication history). Standard determinations are
issued within 72 hours; urgent requests within 24 hours. Once approved, the
pharmacy resubmits the original claim.
