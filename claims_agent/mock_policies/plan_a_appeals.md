# PLAN-A Coverage Determination and Appeals
Document version: 2026.02 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## Level 1 - Coverage Determination
When a PA is denied or a product is excluded, the member or prescriber may
request a coverage determination. Standard requests are decided within 72 hours
of receiving the request; expedited requests within 24 hours where the standard
timeframe could seriously jeopardise the member's health.

## Level 2 - Redetermination
If the Level 1 determination is unfavourable, the member has 60 calendar days
from the notice date to request a redetermination. This is reviewed by a
clinician who was not involved in the original decision.

## Formulary Exception
A prescriber may request a formulary exception for a non-covered product by
submitting a statement that all covered alternatives would be less effective,
or would cause an adverse reaction for this member. Approved exceptions are
typically granted at the Tier 4 cost share.

## What Cannot Be Appealed
Claim rejections that are data or process errors are not appeals. Reject code 79
(refill too soon) is resolved through the override process, not an appeal.
Reject code 88 (DUR) is resolved by pharmacist review, not an appeal. An unknown
or malformed reject code indicates a submission error and should be corrected
and resubmitted by the pharmacy.
