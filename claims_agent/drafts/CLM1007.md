### Claim CLM1007 — Draft Resolution Summary

**What happened:**
Member Priya Nair (M101, PLAN-A, active) had a claim for **Oxycodone 5 mg tab (30-day supply, Qty 30, Prescriber: Dr. B. Shah)** rejected at point of sale.

---

**Why it rejected:**
Reject code **79 — Refill Too Soon.** The member attempted to refill the prescription before the plan-permitted refill window had opened. Per PLAN-A policy, a standard 30-day supply may be refilled only after **day 23** (75% of days supply elapsed). More critically, **Oxycodone is a Schedule II controlled substance**, and PLAN-A requires **100% of the days supply to elapse** before a refill is permitted — meaning the earliest eligible refill date is **day 30**. No early counter-level override is available for Schedule II drugs. *(plan_a_refill_policy.md, v2026.02)*

---

**Recommended fix:**
Because this is a Schedule II controlled substance, the standard refill-too-soon override **cannot** be applied at the pharmacy counter. The claims specialist should:

1. **Check the reject message** for the exact earliest eligible fill date returned by the plan adjudicator.
2. **If the member has a valid override reason** (e.g., lost/stolen medication, dose change, travel, or transition of care), initiate a **plan review**:
   - *Lost/Stolen:* Obtain a police report or signed member attestation before submitting for review.
   - *Dose change:* Confirm a new prescription is on file from Dr. B. Shah.
   - *Other reasons:* Contact the **PLAN-A pharmacy help desk** with the claim number and documented override reason.
3. **Prescriber attestation is required** for Schedule II early refill overrides — coordinate with Dr. B. Shah's office as needed.
4. If no valid override reason exists, **advise the pharmacy/member to resubmit on or after the eligible refill date**.
